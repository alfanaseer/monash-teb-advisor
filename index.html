        • • Summer 2028 vs. Final Semester (Sem 1, 2029) Underload Alert: On your attached course map, taking SCI3800 — Science internship in Summer 2028 leaves only 3 units (18 credit points) in your final semester (Sem 1, 2029: BIO3800, BIO3810, SCI3990).
