# Monash University Malaysia — BSc (Tropical Environmental Biology) Course & Elective Advisor

Interactive single-page Course & Career Advisor web application for **S2000 — Bachelor of Science** (**TRENVBIO05 / TRENVBIO06 — Tropical environmental biology**) students at **Monash University Malaysia** (July / Semester 2, 2026 Intake Cohort).

## Features
1. **Interactive Career & Pathway Quiz:** Evaluates student preferences across lab workload, assessment style, nutrition/pharma interests, and career profile to recommend a tailored 2027–2029 study plan.
2. **2026–2029 Course Map & WES Simulator:** Live progression tracker enforcing the **10-unit (60 CP) Level 1 maximum cap**, **36 CP Level 1 Science minimum**, **60 CP Level 2/3 Science minimum**, Malaysia Semester 1 vs. Semester 2 availability, and unit prerequisites.
3. **Complete Unit Explorer (32 Verified Units):** Searchable catalog of the 9 original shortlisted units plus 23 Biology, Biotechnology, Food Science, Statistics, and Humanities (`AMU`) units (excluding Psychology) with direct links to the [Monash University Handbook](https://handbook.monash.edu/).
4. **International Student Visa, General Studies (9410) & Honours Checklist:** Tracks compulsory Malaysian Ministry of Higher Education MPU units (`MPU3143`, `MPU3172`, `MPU3312`, `MPU3222`, `MPU3322`, `MPU3412`), 4-unit student pass load rules, and `S3701 — Bachelor of Science (Honours)` eligibility.

## How to Publish on GitHub Pages
1. Upload `index.html` and `README.md` to the root of a public GitHub repository (e.g., `monash-teb-advisor`).
2. In your GitHub repository, navigate to **Settings → Pages**.
3. Under **Build and deployment**, select **Deploy from a branch**, choose **`main`** and **`/ (root)`**, and click **Save**.
4. Your site will be live within 60 seconds at `https://<your-github-username>.github.io/monash-teb-advisor/`.
